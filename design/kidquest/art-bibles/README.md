## Shared rules (all themes, pipeline §10.1, §11)

- Friendly, optimistic, tactile, rounded; readable for children aged 6–9 at **38 px per cell**.
- One light: **upper-left**, soft. No baked ground shadows (shared CSS owns them, pipeline §11.2).
- Ink outlines use the theme's `--kq-ink`; minimum 2.5 px at @2x on props and icons so silhouettes survive 38 px.
- **Geometry is shared and never themed**: cell 128 × 128 px @2x; road band x/y 34–93 (48 px surface + 6 px border each side) centred on every connected edge midpoint; open road sides empty within 8 px of the edge; shore land strip 18 px, bank to 28 px, foam after; quest board 128 × 192 px, anchor bottom-centre, contact line y 184. See [`../templates/geometry.json`](../templates/geometry.json).
- No text, numbers, letters, currency, logos or flags baked into art. Notices on boards are blank shapes.
- No weapons, danger, ruins, real national or religious symbols, brands, advertising, cars or traffic, money cues, or caricature of people, regions or ethnic dress.
- Terrain stays calm and low-noise, so semantic overlays (focus, selection, valid, invalid, connectivity) read on top. Overlays are shared SVG and never baked into art.
- Water is calm and friendly: no waves breaking, no dark depth, no creatures.

## Review checklist (to be completed by humans, not by this task)

- [ ] Art review: palette, silhouettes, line weight and lighting consistency, 38/48/64 proofs.
- [ ] Cultural review (Kampung especially): respectful, contemporary, not primitive or impoverished, not tied to one regional costume or a single ethnic group's sacred motif.
- [ ] Accessibility review of the overlays on each theme's ground (contrast ≥ 3:1 for UI, non-colour cue present).
- [ ] Child comprehension check: the quest board and roads read as such at 38 px.

# Kerajaan Ceria (`kingdom`)

Status: draft. **Pending art review and pending cultural review.** Golden reference art is generated, not illustrated.

## Intent

A friendly storybook civic kingdom: warm stone, timber, coloured bunting, rounded towers, small
gardens and welcoming arches. A place for everyone, not a fortress and not a class ladder.

## Material rules

- Stone is warm and rounded; blocks have soft highlights on the upper-left and a soft lower-right shade.
- Banners are bunting (triangles) in berry and gold. **Never** a real or realistic flag, coat of arms, crown-on-shield heraldry or any national colour combination used as a flag.
- Towers are rounded and short, with open arches and windows. No battlements used for defence, no arrow slits, no gates with portcullis.

## Golden reference treatments (KQ-006)

- **Quest board — Papan Berita Kerajaan (Herald Board):** two stone pillars, timber board with parchment notices and a gold seal, blue arched crest with gold trim and berry/gold bunting.
- **Wood icon:** three logs end-on with a small blue ribbon bow.
- **Thumbnail:** composed from the theme's own ground, shore, road and board.

## Prohibited

- Weapons, armour, soldiers, battle or siege architecture, dungeons, ruins, dragons or threats.
- Thrones or crowns as rewards, "royal versus commoner" status cues.
- Real flags, heraldry, religious symbols, text, money.

# Kampung Ceria (`perkampungan`, default theme)

Status: draft. **Pending art review and pending cultural review.** Golden reference art is generated, not illustrated.

## Intent

A warm, lived-in, contemporary Indonesian village: timber, bamboo details, clay roof tiles, stone
footings, planted edges, woven accents, baskets and shared community space. Cared for and active,
never primitive, poor or "exotic". Not tied to one region, one ethnic group or one traditional costume.

## Material rules

- Timber is warm mid-brown with a lighter upper-left edge; bamboo and woven panels use diagonal weave lines, never a specific regional textile pattern.
- Roofs: clay tiles in rows, terracotta, simple gable. No specific sacred or palace roof form (no *joglo* crown, no *tongkonan* boat roof, no *rumah gadang* horns) unless a cultural reviewer approves it.
- Stone appears as rounded footings and pebbles, not as monuments.
- Plants: leafy and generic (banana leaf, grass tufts); no offerings, no religious objects.

## Golden reference treatments (KQ-006)

- **Quest board — Papan Pengumuman:** two timber posts on stone footings, woven bamboo panel with two blank paper notices and coloured pins, small clay-tile roof.
- **Wood icon:** three logs end-on with a two-leaf sprig.
- **Thumbnail:** composed from the theme's own ground, shore, road and board.

## Prohibited

- Poverty or decay cues (patched roofs, rubbish, broken fences), "primitive" framing, grass skirts or any costume.
- Religious buildings or symbols, offerings, flags, political or ethnic emblems.
- Text on signs, prices, money, shop brands.

# Kota Ceria (`city`)

Status: draft. **Pending art review and pending cultural review.** Golden reference art is generated, not illustrated.

## Intent

A bright, clean, contemporary civic town for people on foot: simple masonry, safe pedestrian paving,
awnings, planters, community gardens, broad windows and visible entrances.

## Material rules

- Paving is a light running bond; curbs are light grey with a darker outer line so the path reads on the lawn.
- Planters and greenery soften every civic object.
- Colour comes from awnings, frames and planters, not from signage.

## Golden reference treatments (KQ-006)

- **Quest board — Kios Info Kota (Civic Kiosk):** teal rounded kiosk frame on a grey post, light panel with four blank coloured notice cards, coral-and-white striped awning, planter base.
- **Wood icon:** three sawn planks with a coral strap (same overall mass as the log stacks).
- **Thumbnail:** composed from the theme's own ground, shore, road and board.

## Prohibited

- Cars, traffic, traffic lights, car roads, parking, pollution, construction danger.
- Advertising, logos, brand colours, shop names, prices, ATMs or money.
- Text or letters on signs (the "i" information glyph is also avoided).
